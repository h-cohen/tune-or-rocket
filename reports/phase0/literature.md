# Phase 0 literature report (paper 2: fine-tuned TSFM vs training-free classification)

Search date: 2026-11-20. Merged from four note files (new-work search, the three unread papers, R2R/UniShape/NuTime/Reverso access, workshop and Scholar gap search). Q1-Q6 are the sub-questions of NEXT-PAPER-BRIEF.md section 3.3.

Rules used in this report: numbers are copied from the notes with their source; none were added. Labels "unverified", "abstract only" and "via summariser" are kept. A fetch tool that summarises pages ("summariser") was used for most HTML reading, so every number marked "full text (HTML, via summariser)" is the tool's extraction, not a raw read. Where notes conflict, later corrections are used (listed in section 0).

## 0. Corrections applied over the notes

- Mantis v1 pretraining corpus (arXiv 2502.15637v1, Sec. 2.4): a 7M-series union that includes UCR and UEA (minus EigenWorms and InsectWingbeat). The v2 text says 1.89M. These two are unreconciled. Do not state one figure for Mantis without naming the version.
- Jain et al. (2611.06988), App. B: final encoder states are pooled over patch tokens, excluding the summary token; mean and max pooling are reported; only Chronos-Bolt base is used (no other Chronos size).
- Reverso: shinfxh/reverso-base was measured at 2,609,585 parameters, matching the paper's "2.6M". The mapping to the paper's 2.6M model is inferred from the config match. This supersedes the earlier note that the 2.6M weights were possibly not public (that note was from the GitHub README; the Hugging Face repo name was not known then).
- Isiacik and Ifrim NeurIPS 2026 / arXiv 2610.30950: one note had abstract only, the later search read the full arXiv HTML (via summariser). Full-text status is used here.
- Isiacik et al. ECML 2025: one note had abstract only; the later note read the main text (18 pp, no appendix) from the ECML preprint PDF. Full-text status (main text) is used here.

## 5. Overall novelty statement

Statement (brief wording): no controlled comparison of this kind exists, where "this kind" means several fine-tuned TSFMs (classification-pretrained and forecasting-pretrained) against the strongest training-free pipelines, with a from-scratch control for every FM, several label budgets from few to all, a fixed and equal tuning budget, several benchmarks including domain-shift settings, and measured compute. It should not be worded as "nobody fine-tuned FMs against ROCKET".

Evidence for (in the sources searched, see section 1 for limits):
- No new paper found that does a fine-tuned-TSFM vs ROCKET-family comparison with scratch control, label budgets and cost. No HIGH-threat item. No paper found in the partial TS-LIMITS list, the one reachable FMLTS paper, or the arXiv searches.
- Each close paper misses at least three of the required properties: Jain et al. (one tiny forecaster, 3 datasets, 2 budgets, one seed); R2R and UniShape (full labels, no cost, no budget curve; UniShape scratch ablation on 18 datasets only); Isiacik et al. ECML (4 datasets, single run, no control); Li et al. (one domain, no cost); Isiacik and Ifrim (frozen only).
- No paper found fine-tuning Chronos, Chronos-Bolt, Chronos-2, TimesFM or Toto for classification against the ROCKET family (2026-06 onward), no LoRA-on-TSFM classification paper vs the ROCKET family beyond Li et al., and no classification TSFM paper with GPU-hour cost per arm (UniShape appendix F unread).

Evidence against (what the paper must acknowledge and cite):
- UniShape (2601.06429) and R2R (2610.26114): fine-tuned UniShape beats MR-Hydra at full labels on 128 UCR (average rank 4.47 vs 6.27 in R2R; accuracy 0.8708 vs 0.8621 in the UniShape paper), with a from-scratch ablation on 18 datasets.
- Jain et al. already report that fine-tuned Reverso beats its random-init twin in 17 of 18 runs at 1% yet stays below the best baseline; Li et al. have a label-budget + scratch + LoRA design on one dataset; Isiacik et al. (ECML 2025) is a Mantis-FT vs ROCKET-family comparison on 4 MTS datasets.
- The training-free frontier is moving (TIGER, MASHT, TS2TabPFN, TimEE, ChorusTIC, SwitchPFN); a claim built on a fixed training-free arm can be outdated by submission.
- Coverage limits: OpenReview TS-LIMITS and ACML FMLTS lists, Semantic Scholar and Google Scholar were not accessed; absence of hits is not proof. The search should be re-run before submission, as brief section 5 already requires.
